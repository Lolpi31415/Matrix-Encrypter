Information on TM 32-220

Basic Information

TM 32-220 is the United State Army's training manual for basic cryptography. "Basic Cryptography". It was authorized by order of the Secretary of the Army and signed by Major General Edward F. Witsell, the Adjutant General. The historical document, which includes foundational works associated with famed cryptanalyst William F. Friedman, was later declassified and made available to the public. (NSA)

The training manual goes into the fundamentals of cryptography and starts off with matrix operations (the types found in the encrypter on this site) and later goes into more complicated concepts that I could not care to build with javascript. You can find the full manual online at the internet archive or through the NSA's website. Both will be linked at the bottom of this page right above the sources.

Paperback copies of TM 32-220 are rare but avalible for purchase on various online marketplaces like ebay and similar sites. They are not that expensive with prices ranging from $10-$35+ depending on condition and what the seller things it is worth. The price may be higher because some sellers believe that it is work more due to its historical value and significance with the Korean War.
